Course Overview

Welcome to Business English!

This is a 16-week, fully online course offered by McLennan Community College.

Learning Outcomes & Objectives

Departmental Student Learning Outcome: Using fundamental reading skills, apply basic rules of grammar, spelling, capitalization, number usage, and punctuation, and utilize terminology applicable to technical and business writing and when speaking.


  • Identify and apply the basic rules of grammar, spelling, capitalization, number usage, and punctuation.
  • Utilize terminology applicable to technical and business writing.
  • Develop proofreading and editing skills.
  • Write sentences and paragraphs while structuring sentence types properly.
  • Define and apply gender neutrality to writing content.

Attendance, Verification & Late Policies

Census Date Requirements

To establish official course verification before the census date, you must actively complete and submit at least one formal assignment (such as a syllabus quiz, discussion board post, or first homework entry). Simply logging into Brightspace or sending an introductory email does not record attendance. Failure to submit an assignment before the census date results in an automatic administrative drop.

General Absences & Mid-Term Withdrawal

If you fail to submit two consecutive weeks of module milestones without an officially documented excuse prior to the 60% completion checkpoint of the term, you will be withdrawn. Past the 60% marker, administrative drops cease; you will instead receive the raw cumulative grade earned at term's end.

Late Work Concessions

Strict Policy: Assignments may be turned in up to one week late, subject to a flat 30-point deduction. Submissions pushed beyond this late grace period automatically receive a score of zero.


Note: Filing late work does not bypass attendance frameworks. If you run into an emergency status, contact the instructor immediately to process accommodations.

Generative AI Usage Framework

🚦 Category: Yellow Light (Conditional Use OK)

Generative AI platforms should act strictly as supplemental tools to enhance individual conceptual clarity. They must not replace the direct core writing, analysis, or intellectual workload of this intensive course.

Permitted Assistance

  • Structural outlining and initial brainstorming sessions.
  • Generating external example paradigms for learning.
  • Deconstructing complex assignment briefs.
  • Basic grammar, mechanics, and spelling evaluations.

Prohibited Utilization

  • Directly composing submission emails, major content papers, essays, or coursework modules.
  • Drafting forum discussion replies.
  • Generating answers for tests, unit quizzes, or diagnostics.

Mandatory Transparency Metrics

If you utilize generative engines for allowed baseline research or brainstorming functions, you must strictly document the interactions:

  • Provide the exact textual prompts delivered to the utility.
  • Clearly cite the specific platform version leveraged (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini).

Plagiarism Alert: Copying/pasting un-attributed AI outputs constitutes academic fraud and receives a zero on the first offense.

Academic Integrity & Behavioral Norms

Academic Dishonesty Definitions

Plagiarism: Passing off someone else's concepts, writing structures, or AI generation profiles without full explicit citation frameworks.

Cheating: Swapping evaluation parameters with peers, relying on illicit text resources during assessments, or collaborating on individual assignments.


Disciplinary Trajectory:

  • First Infraction: Immediate zero (0) grade on the affected submission alongside a documented warning notice.
  • Second Infraction: Formal escalation directly to the Student Discipline Committee for comprehensive institutional review.

Behavioral Standards

Students are expected to communicate respectfully and professionally, ensuring correct mechanics and respectful phrasing are preserved within all virtual environments. Displays of disruptive behavior will result in an immediate drop from the course roster and a student conduct referral.
